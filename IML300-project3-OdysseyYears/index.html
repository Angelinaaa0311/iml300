THE ODYSSEY YEARS

This is a warm interactive web platform dedicated to young people in the Odyssey Years, the confusing transitional stage of life filled with anxiety, loneliness and self-exploration. Taking "life's rainy season" as the core visual theme, the homepage adopts a gentle rainy animation and briefly interprets the concept of the Odyssey Years, with five interactive umbrellas as the entry to five caring modules. The platform integrates delicate animations and human-computer interaction to provide spiritual comfort: the white noise module offers soothing sounds for meditation and relaxation; the tree-planting module allows users to nurture a small tree, witnessing inner growth with encouraging words; the gift box module brings random interesting trivial things to fight emptiness; the Q&A module guides users to explore themselves through ten heart-to-heart questions; the Firebase-based message board realizes anonymous sharing of worries and mutual comfort. It aims to accompany young people through the confused rainy period of life, convey gentle care, and build a warm networked care space for self-growth and emotional companionship.
