Definition

Odyssey usually refers to a period of exploration,

trial and error, and wandering,

but meaningful transition in life.

It is commonly seen among young people

in their twenties to early thirties.

During this period, people may not yet have

a stable career, partner, or life direction.

Instead, they keep trying different jobs, cities,

and relationships.

Through setbacks and growth,

they gradually clarify their self-identity

and life goals.
