Project 1: Interactive Audio & Visual with p5.js

I have always believed that sound, or rather music, can preserve memories. Whenever I revisit tunes I heard during a particular period, they invariably evoke the sensations and emotions of that time. That is why I created this project. It is a book, my personal diary, chronicling the twelve months of 2025. 2025 holds profound significance for me—it marked my most crucial application season, my 18th birthday coming-of-age ceremony, and my first solo journey abroad for studies. Thus, I selected the most representative photographs and a song capable of evoking memories for each month. We can interactively explore each page. May the code grant my happiness eternal preservation.

Project 2: HyperNarrative

This project is a small interactive website inspired by story-based games. Players take on the role of the main character joining a fictional dating show, and make different choices throughout several days of the show. Based on all the decisions they make, the website will show their personality type in relationships, who they are most compatible with, and what kind of person they are in love. I made this project because I want to create something fun and relaxing, while also letting people learn a little more about themselves through a lighthearted game.
