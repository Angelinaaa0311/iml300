Project 1: Interactive Audio & Visual with p5.js

I have always believed that sound, or rather music, can preserve memories. Whenever I revisit tunes I heard during a particular period, they invariably evoke the sensations and emotions of that time. That is why I created this project. It is a book, my personal diary, chronicling the twelve months of 2025. 2025 holds profound significance for me—it marked my most crucial application season, my 18th birthday coming-of-age ceremony, and my first solo journey abroad for studies. Thus, I selected the most representative photographs and a song capable of evoking memories for each month. We can interactively explore each page. May the code grant my happiness eternal preservation.

My 2025 memoir

Project 2: HyperNarrative

This project is a small interactive website inspired by story-based games. Players take on the role of the main character joining a fictional dating show, and make different choices throughout several days of the show. Based on all the decisions they make, the website will show their personality type in relationships, who they are most compatible with, and what kind of person they are in love. I made this project because I want to create something fun and relaxing, while also letting people learn a little more about themselves through a lighthearted game.

Crush Island

Project 3: Networked Care

This is a warm interactive web platform for young people in the Odyssey Years, a confusing transitional life stage full of anxiety and loneliness. With "life's rainy season" as its visual theme, the homepage features a gentle rain animation and five interactive umbrellas leading to five healing modules: a white noise relax room, a growing tree companion, a random gift box, a self-exploration quiz, and a Firebase-powered message board. It aims to accompany users through confused days and build a warm networked care space.

Vibe Coding Exercise #8

Thoughts

Overall, this semester felt like opening the door to a brand-new world. Through constant practice and learning new knowledge here, I gradually grew from a shy, inexperienced beginner into someone more mature and confident.

I still remember my first class. I had zero background in coding and never took any computer or programming courses in high school. I was totally confused and extremely nervous at the very beginning, and I even wanted to drop this course.

Later, I went to office hours to ask questions and communicate with professors and TAs, which helped me ease my fears and dare to have a try. At first, I felt really anxious and stressed to present my projects in front of the whole class. Yet by listening to feedback and suggestions from teachers and classmates, I gained diverse perspectives and valuable advice. Regular in-class presentations also greatly polished my expressive and communication skills.

I can clearly feel that with more practice and every finished project, my works have become more professional, and I have grown fonder of what I create. Gradually, I even fell in love with coding. I am willing to spend hours working on it, and I no longer feel timid or hesitant to try new things.

I truly love this course. I hope all the knowledge and experience I have gained here can accompany me in my future study and life.
